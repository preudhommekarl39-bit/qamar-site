Walk-in blow-dry in Dubai, without an appointment

Most Dubai salons ask you to book days ahead, then run late anyway. QAMAR works the other way round: you walk in, join the queue from your phone, and sit down when your chair is ready.

How walk-in works at QAMAR

  • Join the queue from your phone when you arrive: you see your place in line.
  • Wait with a drink, or step out for a few minutes. Your phone tells you the moment your chair is ready.
  • Choose your look in the chair. Every look on the menu takes 45 minutes or less.
  • Going straight out? Add a private shower while your outfit, abaya or hijab is steamed and perfumed.

Why walk-in instead of booking

A booking makes sense for colour or a big change of cut. A blow-dry is different: you usually decide on the day, once you know the weather, your plans and how your hair behaves. Walk-in means no deposit, no reminder messages, and no penalty when a meeting runs over and you arrive late.

Frequently asked questions

No. QAMAR is walk-in only. You join the queue from your phone when you arrive, and your phone tells you when your chair is ready.

Every look takes 45 minutes or less. Qamar Express is done in 35 minutes, or it is on us.
