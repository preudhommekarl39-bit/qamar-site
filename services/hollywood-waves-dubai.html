Hollywood waves and curls in Dubai

Marina Waves is the QAMAR look for volume and movement: soft beach waves for a brunch, or polished Hollywood curls for a dinner, an engagement or a photo shoot.

Beach waves or Hollywood curls?

  • Beach waves are looser and a little undone, with straighter ends. Easy for daytime and casual plans.
  • Hollywood curls are brushed out into even, glossy waves that fall to one side. The classic look for evenings and weddings.
  • Not sure? Show your stylist a photo of the outfit. The neckline often decides: open necklines suit side-swept curls, high necks suit volume at the back.

Waves and Dubai humidity

Waves drop faster in humidity than a sleek finish. Two habits help: let the curls cool completely before they are brushed out, and keep a claw clip in your bag for the walk outside. If you will spend the evening outdoors between May and October, ask for slightly tighter curls that relax into waves during the night, or choose a sleek blowout instead.

Under a shayla or hijab

Curls flatten under a hijab, so many women prefer gentle volume at the crown and waves only in the lengths, for the moment they uncover at a ladies-only event. Tell your stylist if you will cover your hair after the salon and how you pin your shayla.
