A hair wash and care ritual in Dubai

Dubai is hard on hair: strong sun, pool and sea water, dusty days and air-conditioning from morning to night. The Moon Ritual is 30 minutes of care at the wash basin, on its own or before your blow-dry.

What the Moon Ritual includes

  • A velvet wash chair and a warm towel around your shoulders.
  • A thorough wash and a nourishing mask.
  • Steam, to help the mask work while you rest.
  • About 30 minutes, walk-in, with an all-female team.

The complete ritual

Going straight out? Add the complete ritual for 79 AED (indicative): a private shower with towel and products, one piece of your outfit steamed and perfumed, a hair perfume, and a coffee or tea. On their own, those extras would cost:

How often to treat your hair

  • Once a week if you swim often or colour your hair.
  • Every two weeks for most hair in the hot months.
  • Before an event, so the blow-dry comes out smoother and shinier.

Club members have the shower, steaming, perfume and a drink included on every visit.

Frequently asked questions

Can I come for a hair wash only?

Yes. The Moon Ritual works on its own: about 30 minutes, 149 AED (indicative), no appointment.

Can I combine it with a blow-dry?

Yes. Choose both when you join the queue, and your stylist will tell you the total time.

Is the shower private?

Yes. It is your own shower, with towel and products: 49 AED on its own, or part of the complete ritual.

Which drinks are there?

Coffee, tea, karak, matcha and fresh juice, from the drinks bar.
