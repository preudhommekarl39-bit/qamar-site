A women-only, hijab-friendly salon in Dubai

For many women in Dubai, privacy decides where they get their hair done. QAMAR is women-only from the door to the chair, with an all-female team, so you can take off your hijab or shayla without a second thought.

Privacy, by design

  • Women only: every guest and every member of the team.
  • An all-female team of stylists and hosts.
  • Your own private shower, not a shared changing room.
  • Bilingual service in Arabic and English.

Your abaya, hijab and shayla

While you shower, a second team member steams your abaya, dress or hijab and gives it a light scent, so everything is fresh when you leave. Steam relaxes creases on crepe, nida and chiffon without the shine an iron can leave. At the hijab bar you will also find pins and accessories.

A quiet place to pray

The lounge has a calm prayer room with prayer mats, the qibla direction and a mirror, so you never have to rush your blow-dry around prayer time.

Covering after your blow-dry

  • Ask for volume at the crown, so the shape shows gently under the shayla.
  • Let the hair cool completely before covering, or it will flatten.
  • A hair perfume (oud, rose or white musk) stays fresh under the hijab for hours.

Frequently asked questions

Are there men in the salon?

No. QAMAR is women-only, for guests and team.

Can my abaya and hijab be steamed?

Yes. While you shower, your dress, abaya or hijab is steamed (35 AED per piece, indicative) and can be lightly perfumed. Club members have it included.

Is there a prayer room?

Yes, with prayer mats, the qibla direction and a mirror.

Can I bring my daughter?

Yes. Mini Glow is a mini blow-dry or braids for girls, 25 minutes, 89 AED (indicative).
