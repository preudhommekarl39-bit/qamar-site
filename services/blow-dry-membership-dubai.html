A blow-dry membership in Dubai whose price goes down

If you get your hair done every week, paying visit by visit adds up quickly. The QAMAR Club turns it into one monthly price with the extras included, and that price goes down every month you stay.

The three plans

PlanBlow-driesIndicative price (Month 1)
Crescent2 blow-dries a month329 AED / month
Half Moon1 blow-dry a week599 AED / month
Full MoonUp to 3 blow-dries a week1199 AED / month

How the price drops

Your monthly price falls by 2% for every month you stay, until it reaches −16% in your 9th month. After that it stays at that level for as long as you remain a member. With the indicative prices, that looks like this:

PlanMonth 1Month 4Month 9 onwards
Crescent329 AED309 AED276 AED
Half Moon599 AED563 AED503 AED
Full Moon1199 AED1127 AED1007 AED

The minimum commitment is 3 months. You can pause up to 2 months a year and keep your price level. The first 100 members are planned to start directly at the month-4 price.

What every member gets

  • Members’ lane in the queue
  • Your price goes down every month you stay
  • A friend at −50% once a month
  • Birthday blow-dry on us
  • An unused visit carries over to next month

Is it worth it?

With Half Moon, a blow-dry a week costs about 150 AED per visit from the first month, with the shower, steaming and a drink included. Paid one by one, a Qamar Glow blow-dry with the complete ritual comes to 258 AED. If you come twice a month or more, a plan usually makes sense.

Prices are indicative, in AED, and will be confirmed before opening.

  • Women only, all-female team, Arabic and English.

Frequently asked questions

How does the QAMAR Club price drop work?

Each month you stay, your price falls by 2%, until −16% from the 9th month. It then stays there.

Is there a minimum commitment?

3 months. After that you can cancel at any time, and you can pause up to 2 months a year.

What if I miss a visit?

An unused visit carries over to the next month.

Are the Club prices final?

Not yet. They are indicative and will be confirmed before the March 2027 opening.
