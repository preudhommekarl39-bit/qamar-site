How to make a blow-dry last in Dubai humidity

From May to October, Dubai humidity can undo a fresh blow-dry in the time it takes to walk from the car to the restaurant. Here is what helps.

Choose a style that holds

  • Sleek and straight lasts longest, as long as the hair is fully dry at the root.
  • A bouncy round-brush blow-dry keeps its shape better than loose waves.
  • Defined curls set with a diffuser handle humidity well because they already have their natural shape.

What to ask your stylist

  • Dry the roots completely. Damp roots are the first place frizz starts.
  • Use a light anti-humidity spray at the end, not a heavy oil that weighs the hair down.
  • Finish with a cool shot of air to set the shape.

During the evening

  • Keep a small claw clip in your bag for the walk outside.
  • Do not touch your hair too often. Hands add moisture.
  • Go from car to inside quickly in summer: five minutes outdoors at night can be enough to lift the roots.
