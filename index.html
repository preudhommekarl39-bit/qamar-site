Questions

Everything you want to know.

Do I need to book?

No. QAMAR is walk-in only: when you arrive, scan the code at the entrance or open this website, pick your look and follow your place in line on your phone.

How long does a blow-dry take?

Every look on the menu takes 45 minutes or less. Qamar Express takes 35 minutes, SOS 15 takes 15 minutes. Your estimated wait is shown before you join.

How much does a blow-dry cost in Dubai at QAMAR?

Each look on our menu shows an indicative price in AED. Final prices, and whether they include VAT, will be confirmed before opening.

How does the walk-in queue work?

Choose your look and a stylist, or the first one free. The page shows your place and estimated wait, then tells you your seat and stylist when it is your turn, so you can relax, have a matcha or take photos meanwhile.

Which payment methods will you accept?

Card, Apple Pay and cash are planned, with instalments for packs. The final list will be confirmed before opening.

What are your opening hours?

We plan to open every day from 8 AM to midnight. Final opening hours will be confirmed before launch.

Where is QAMAR?

In Dubai. The exact address will be announced here before opening.

How do offers and discounts work?

Only one discount applies per visit: the best one you are eligible for. Discounts apply to the service, never to drinks or tips. Offer conditions will be published before they start.

Do you have services for girls and kids?

Yes: Mini Glow is a mini blow-dry or braids for little girls, in 25 minutes.
