Questions

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in whenever you like. If every chair is busy, your phone shows your place in line.

How long does it take?

45 minutes at most for the full visit: shower, outfit, blow-dry and a drink. A blow-dry alone is quicker.

Is it only for women?

Yes. Our guests and our whole team are women.

Can you steam my abaya or hijab?

Yes. Dresses, abayas, shirts and hijabs are steamed and lightly perfumed while you shower.

What can I drink?

Coffee, tea, matcha, fresh juices and iced drinks, served on a tray at your chair.

How much does it cost?

Prices are published before opening. The membership gives the best price, and it drops the longer you stay.
