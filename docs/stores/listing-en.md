## Description (Apple and Google Play, max 4000)

```text
QAMAR is a walk-in blow-dry lounge in Dubai. There are no appointments: you come when it suits you. This app shows you how busy the lounge is, lets you join the queue before you arrive and tells you when your chair is ready.

SEE THE QUEUE
The home screen shows how many people are waiting, the estimated wait and whether it is a quiet or busy moment, so you can choose when to come.

JOIN FROM YOUR PHONE
Pick your look, say how many of you are coming, choose a stylist or the first one available, and add your first name and WhatsApp number. Your ticket shows your place in line, the estimated wait and the time your chair should be ready.

KNOW WHEN IT IS YOUR TURN
When a stylist calls you, the app turns pink and shows your seat number and your stylist. With notifications on, your phone also alerts you. Not coming right away? Set a reminder for later.

LOOKS AND PRICES
Browse every look with its duration and price: express blow-dry, glow, waves and curls, sleek straight, quick styles, care ritual, cut and colour finishes, and mini looks for girls. You pay at the lounge after your service. There are no payments in the app.

YOUR QAMAR
Your current ticket, notification settings, English or Arabic, the QAMAR Club plans and the bring-a-friend credit, all in one place.

PRIVACY
No account and no password. We only use your first name and number for your place in line. No advertising and no tracking.

The app is available in English and Arabic.
```
