A walk-in blow-dry near DIFC and the Trade Centre

DIFC and the Trade Centre area sit at the eastern end of the stretch where QAMAR opens, off Sheikh Zayed Road. Early meetings, client dinners, conference days: the planned hours, 8 AM to midnight, are made for that rhythm.

Before an early meeting

With a planned opening time of 8 AM, a Qamar Express before a 9 AM meeting is realistic if you are among the first in the queue. It takes 35 minutes, or it is on us.

Exhibition and conference days

When large exhibitions run at Dubai World Trade Centre, the roads around the area fill up quickly, especially in the morning and when the halls close. Leave extra time on those days, or come for your blow-dry before the crowds.

A sleek look for client dinners

Midnight Sleek is the look that holds best through a long evening indoors and the short walk outside. For a dinner after 8 PM, Night Glow gives you any blow-dry with a matcha on us.
