A walk-in blow-dry near Al Barsha and Mall of the Emirates

If you live in Al Barsha or spend your weekends around Mall of the Emirates, QAMAR will be one of the closest walk-in blow-dry options on Sheikh Zayed Road: it opens off the road, a short drive from Al Barsha, towards DIFC.

Getting there from Al Barsha

  • Mall of the Emirates sits at the western end of the stretch where QAMAR opens, so you drive towards DIFC on Sheikh Zayed Road rather than away from the city.
  • There is a Salik toll gate on Sheikh Zayed Road near Al Barsha. Check your route if you prefer to avoid it.
  • At weekends the roads around the mall get busy from late afternoon. A morning blow-dry is the calm option.

Good moments to come

  • Saturday morning, before a family lunch.
  • After the school drop-off on weekdays, during the planned happy hours (10 AM to 3 PM, −20%).
  • Before a mall evening or a cinema night, with Night Glow from 8 PM.
