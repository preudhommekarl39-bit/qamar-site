A women-only walk-in blow-dry near Jumeirah

Jumeirah lies between the beach and Sheikh Zayed Road. From Umm Suqeim, Al Safa or Al Wasl Road, the main road is only a few minutes inland, and QAMAR opens just off it, a short drive from Jumeirah.

From the beach to the evening

Salt water, sun and sand are hard on hair. The Moon Ritual (a wash, a mask and steam in about 30 minutes) followed by a blow-dry brings it back before dinner. Add a private shower while your outfit is steamed and perfumed, and you go straight from the beach to the evening.

Getting there

  • From Umm Suqeim, Al Thanya Road and the other interchanges take you up to Sheikh Zayed Road in a few minutes.
  • Around school times, roughly 7 to 8 AM and early afternoon, the roads near Jumeirah schools are slower. Mid-morning is the easy window.
  • Planned happy hours, 10 AM to 3 PM on weekdays, take 20% off.

With your daughter

Mini Glow is a mini blow-dry or braids for girls, in about 25 minutes, for birthdays, Eid or a school event. It is women-only, so mothers and daughters can both relax.

Do you style children’s hair?

Yes. Mini Glow: a mini blow-dry or braids for girls, 89 AED (indicative).

Can I come straight from the beach?

Yes. Take a private shower, have your outfit steamed, then choose the Moon Ritual or a blow-dry.
