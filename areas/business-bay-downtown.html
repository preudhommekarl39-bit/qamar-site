A walk-in blow-dry near Business Bay and Downtown Dubai

Business Bay and Downtown are where many women in Dubai work, and where many evenings begin. QAMAR opens off Sheikh Zayed Road, a short drive from both, for a blow-dry at lunch or on the way from the office to dinner.

A lunch-break blow-dry

Qamar Express takes 35 minutes, or it is on us, and SOS 15 takes fifteen. During the planned happy hours, 10 AM to 3 PM on weekdays, blow-dries are 20% off.

From the office to dinner

  • Leave the office a little before the evening rush if you can: Sheikh Zayed Road and the roads around Downtown are heaviest between 5 and 8 PM.
  • Bring your outfit in a bag. It is steamed and perfumed while you take a private shower.
  • From 8 PM, Night Glow gives you any blow-dry with a matcha on us.

Every week, for less

If you have a blow-dry every week for meetings and presentations, the QAMAR Club Half Moon plan covers one a week with the extras included, and the price drops 2% every month you stay, down to −16%.
