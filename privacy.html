Privacy policy

Last updated: to be completed at opening.

What we collect

  • Your first name, so your stylist can greet you.
  • Your WhatsApp number, only to tell you your place in line and when it is your turn.
  • Your visit: the look you chose, times, the amount paid and an optional rating or comment.

We never collect or store card numbers or security codes: card and Apple Pay payments are handled by our payment provider.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your number.
  • We do not send marketing messages unless you clearly agree to receive them.
  • Our public welcome screen only shows your first name and initial, never your phone number.

How long we keep it

Proposed rule (to validate): queue details are kept for 30 days, then anonymised. Payment records are kept for as long as accounting law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data by messaging us on WhatsApp or at the lounge. Contact details will be published at opening.

Demo version

The current website is a preview. In this preview, information you type stays in your own browser and is not sent to QAMAR.
